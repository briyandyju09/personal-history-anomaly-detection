# Poster content plan

Canvas A0 landscape (1189 x 841 mm), template `landscape_4col`, token pack `generic` (slate blue + gold). Type tokens `--fs-1..9` are scaled by 0.82 for the narrower A0 canvas (template targets 1524 mm).

| Region | Content | Components |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Title, subtitle, author, affiliation | title-block |
| Banner | Question + four headline numbers | framework-banner |
| Col 1 | 1 Motivation, 2 Protocol, 3 Three data layers, 4 Limits and ethics | card, callout, flow-strip, result-table |
| Col 2 | 5 Sufficiency point (Fig. 1), 6 Three definitions, 7 Recency beats volume | figure-card, keybox, result-table |
| Col 3 | 8 Crowd or person (Fig. 2, hybrid equation), 9 Calibration | figure-card, eqn, eqn-anatomy, callout, result-table |
| Col 4 | 10 Second dataset (Fig. 4), 11 Variability (Fig. 3) | figure-card |
| Strip / footer | Four takeaways; author, affiliation, contact | takeaways-strip, footer |

Figures are the paper's own four (cropped from `paper/main.pdf` at 450 DPI; see `FIGURE_MANIFEST.json`). No logos. Header QR code links to https://briyan.xyz/research.
